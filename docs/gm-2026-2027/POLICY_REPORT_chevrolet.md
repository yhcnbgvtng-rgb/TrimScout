## Judgment calls

- `4WD (vs 2WD)` / `AWD (vs FWD)` rows are CSV "Powertrain" upgrades with a price, so they are kept as powertrain options (a feed line `4WD` / `AWD` matches). They are drivetrain choices as much as upgrades; if Paul wants them treated as configuration (as Ford's 4x4 is), drop the Drivetrain subcategory in `classify`.
- Default-selected / default-included paid items (the CSV notes `default-selected paid paint`, `default-included`, `auto-included`) are kept at their price: the trim's base price leaves them out. Rows:
