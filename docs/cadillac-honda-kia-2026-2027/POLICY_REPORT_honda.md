## Trim handling (nothing is aliased)

- no trim aliases are approved: a trim string that is not a CSV trim (after noise words) is untrusted and gets no options

- Cadillac trims carry the drivetrain in the CSV name (`Sport AWD`, `Luxury 4WD`): a vehicle trim must carry it too (a bare `Sport` on CT5 is untrusted, it could be RWD or AWD); XT5 trims are plain.
