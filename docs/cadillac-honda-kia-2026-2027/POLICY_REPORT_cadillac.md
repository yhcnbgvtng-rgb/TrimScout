## Trim handling (nothing is aliased)

- no trim aliases are approved: a trim string that is not a CSV trim (after noise words) is untrusted and gets no options
- drivetrain / body words (`AWD`, `4WD`, `Crew Cab`, `Short Bed`...) and status words (`Demo`, `Fleet`, `In Transit`) are ignored when matching a trim
- Cadillac trims carry the drivetrain in the CSV name (`Sport AWD`, `Luxury 4WD`): a vehicle trim must carry it too (a bare `Sport` on CT5 is untrusted, it could be RWD or AWD); XT5 trims are plain. Kia `price by drivetrain` pairs are one entry, priced by the drivetrain word in the vehicle trim (default: the FWD price)
